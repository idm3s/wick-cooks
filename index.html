The Vision

Breaking Bread, Building Legacy

The Brunswick School Class of 2015 has always found its way back to the table. From class snacks in lower school to the snack bar in middle school. From food fairs and fundraisers to team dinners and the warmth of a friend's kitchen after practice. Our class found something rare in a world that moves too fast — we slowed down to eat together.

Wick Cooks turns that tradition into something permanent: a hardcover, coffee-table-quality cookbook featuring the family recipes of all 93 graduating families. Each family contributes their signature dishes — Hindy Family Flank Steak, May Lemon Bars — alongside dedication pages that tell the stories behind the food: when it was most enjoyed, why it matters, and who it reminds them of.

This is not just a cookbook. It is a fundraiser for Brunswick, a reunion of flavors, and a lasting tribute to the people who shaped our class.

The Team: Lynn May & Faten Hindy (co-founders & project leads), with the aid of their sons — Sam (design & mock-ups) and Matt (alumni connections & outreach).

Book Specifications

A Book Built for the Kitchen and the Living Room

Wick Cooks is designed to hold up on a kitchen counter and look beautiful on a coffee table. The physical quality is modeled after the classic Brunswick address directory — with the school emblem centered in the interior — but elevated with modern cookbook finishes.

How It Works

Two Types of Pages, One Beautiful Book

Each of the 93 families is invited to contribute recipes and dedications. Families may purchase as few or as many pages as they wish. This model raises funds through the page contributions themselves, with additional revenue from book sales.

Page Type A
Recipe Page
$25 – $35
A full page featuring a family recipe with a title like “Hindy Family Flank Steak” or “May Lemon Bars.” Includes the recipe, a family photo or food image, and the family name.
Page Type B
Dedication Page
$50
A personal page with a write-up, photos (old or new), and a story about the dish, when it was most enjoyed, or a tribute to what the meal means to the family. A keepsake.
Expected range per family: 1–3 recipe pages + 1–2 dedication pages. This gives each family a total contribution of $25–$205, keeping participation accessible while allowing those who want to contribute more a way to do so.

What the Funds Could Become

Tiered Impact Goals

Depending on the total amount raised, the fundraiser unlocks different levels of lasting impact for Brunswick:

If We Raise $5,000–$10,000

A Dedication & Equipment Gift

A meaningful contribution to the school — perhaps a dedicated bench, a piece of equipment for a team, or a named gift to a department that shaped us. Something permanent with a plaque.

If We Raise $10,000–$20,000

A Dedication to Tom Phillip

A commissioned statue, portrait, or lasting tribute honoring Tom Phillip — placed somewhere on the Brunswick campus where future classes can know his name and his spirit.

If We Raise $20,000+

The Tom Phillip Scholarship

An endowed scholarship in Tom’s name, awarded annually to a Brunswick student. The dream outcome. If Wick Cooks becomes a template that other graduating classes adopt — each year running their own fundraising vector — the scholarship could become self-sustaining and grow over time.

In Memory
This Book Is Dedicated To

Tom Phillip

Tom is part of the tapestry that is Brunswick School. His presence has defined our hallways, our fields, and our gatherings. How dearly he will be missed, and how the school will never be the same.

Wick Cooks carries his name in its heart. Every recipe shared, every story told, every page turned is a way of honoring the people who make our community what it is — and the table is always better when everyone has a seat.

The final book will feature a full dedication page in Tom’s honor.
