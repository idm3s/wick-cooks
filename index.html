The Vision

Breaking Bread, Building Legacy

The Brunswick School Class of 2015 has always found its way back to the table. Whether it was class snacks in lower school, the snack bar in middle school, Corbo’s filled lunches, the senior snack lounge binges, food fairs and fundraisers, team dinners, or just the warmth of a friend’s kitchen after practice — food was at the very center of our Brunswick living experience. Our class found something rare in a world that moves too fast: we slowed down to eat together.

Wick Cooks turns that tradition into something permanent: a hardcover, coffee-table-quality cookbook featuring the family recipes of all 93 graduating families. Each family contributes their signature dishes — Hindy Family Flank Steak, May Lemon Bars — alongside dedication pages that tell the stories behind the food: when it was most enjoyed, why it matters, and who it reminds them of.

This is not just a cookbook. It is a fundraiser with a $50,000 goal, a reunion of flavors, and a lasting tribute to the people who shaped our class. Because of how Headmaster Phillip represented our school, made us feel a part of the tapestry, and carried on its ambition as fearless leader — some of the guys from 2015 feel it is only right to come together and support him in his leaving. This is our generous and eager spirit of giving, as an ode and a set of living investments that create the most valuable thing in life: pure, loving, life-filled memories.

The Team: Lynn May & Faten Hindy (co-founders & project leads), with the aid of their sons — Sam (design & mock-ups) and Matt (alumni connections & outreach).

In Memory
This Book Is Dedicated To

Headmaster Tom Phillip

With all our getting, we hope we got understanding. And that means knowing when it is time to joyously step up and send off someone with the orbit of Headmaster Tom Phillip.

Think of nearly four decades of unprecedented growth. Think of the physical footprint of the school as it stamps itself across the beautiful landscapes of Greenwich. Think of all the meetings, the extracurriculars, the announcements — all that he managed, all that he showed up for, the high standard he lived by, and how he did so with the utmost consistency.

One moment, microcosmic of who he was: in the wake of Hurricane Sandy, the world amiss, roads lined with debris and downed trees — there on the side of the road was this grey figure, lumbering through snow and mess. Running. Maintaining routine when the world had lost its. The only thing missing on him was that patented red line on the body of his jacket.

He is part of the tapestry that is Brunswick School. He made us feel a part of it, and carried on its ambition as fearless leader. Every recipe shared in this book, every story told, every page turned is a way of honoring the people who make our community what it is.

The final book will feature a full dedication page in Tom’s honor.
